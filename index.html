Classic Car Log

Support

Classic Car Log is an app for classic car and street rod owners to keep the full history of each vehicle: repairs and servicing, photos and documents, build log, reminders and notes. Everything is stored privately on your own device.

Need help?
Email classicarlogsupport@gmail.com. Please include your app version (Tools › Settings › About) and your iPhone or iPad model.

Common questions

How do I back up my data?

Open Tools › Export Backup and save the file (it ends in .garagebackup) to Files, iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself. It contains all your vehicles, photos and documents. Make a backup before installing any major update.

How do I move my vehicles to a new device, or use two devices?

Export a backup on the first device, send the file to the second (AirDrop, Files or email) and choose Tools › Import Backup. Importing merges the data: newer edits win, and nothing is duplicated. Automatic syncing between devices is not part of the app yet.

I sold a vehicle. Can I keep its history?

Yes. Swipe the vehicle left on the main list and tap Archive. It moves to Tools › Archived Vehicles with all its history, and you can restore it at any time.

My reminders don't alert me.

Open the iPhone or iPad Settings app, choose Notifications › Classic Car Log and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Reminders alert at 9:00 am on the due date. When you open the app, anything due or overdue is listed on the Reminders Due screen.

Can I share a vehicle's details with a parts supplier?

Yes. On the vehicle page tap the share icon and choose Vehicle Details PDF. You choose which details (VIN, build date, engine number and so on) go into the PDF.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a vehicle in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it holds on that device. We never hold a copy of your data.
