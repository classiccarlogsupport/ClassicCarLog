Privacy Policy

Classic Car Log · Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: Classic Car Log keeps your information on your own device. We do not collect it, see it, sell it or share it.

What the app stores

Everything you enter (vehicle details, repair and service records, events, to-do items, build log entries, reminders, notes, photos and documents) is stored only on your device, inside the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server run by us or anyone else. No data is transmitted from the app.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications: to alert you when a reminder you set is due. These are created on your device.
  • Photos and Files: only the photos and documents you choose to attach are copied into the app. The app does not browse your library.

Backups and sharing

If you export a backup or share a PDF, the file is created on your device and goes only where you send it (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Mail or Messages). From that point it is handled by the service you chose and is outside the app's control.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from that device. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to manage.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, adding cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: classicarlogsupport@gmail.com
